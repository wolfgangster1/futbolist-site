Selection Criteria

We're looking for players
ready to compete.

Unsigned players with the quality, fitness, and commitment to perform in a demanding team environment.

  • Professional experience or a strong high-level playing background
  • Currently a free agent and seeking a club
  • Ready to train and compete at a demanding standard
  • Willing to be part of a filmed football project

Applications are reviewed individually. Selected players will be contacted with next steps.

FAQ

A few things to know.

Who can apply?

Any unsigned player with a professional or strong high-level background who's ready to train and compete.

What happens after I submit my profile?

We review every application individually. If you're a match for what the squad needs, you'll be invited to the next step.

Does applying guarantee selection?

No. Applying does not guarantee selection or a professional contract — only an evaluation.

Which matches are confirmed?

We're building the schedule now. A matchup in Pittsburgh is already locked in, with more to follow.
